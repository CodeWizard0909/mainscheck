e-governance is a very important tool in today's world. It means using computers and
internet for government work. It has many benefits for the citizens and also for the
government.

It has increased transparency because information is available online. It has also
increased accountability of officers. Many schemes like {{f:Digital India|Make in
India}} have been launched by the government for this purpose. The
{{f:UMANG app|BHIM app}} provides many services in one place.

But there are many inadequacies also. Internet is not available everywhere. Many
people do not know how to use computers. Sometimes the servers do not work properly.
Cyber attacks are also a very big problem nowadays.

Therefore the government should improve the infrastructure. Training should be given
to the people. Awareness programmes should be conducted in villages. If these steps
are taken then e-governance will be successful in
{{f:India|Bharat Sarkar}} and the citizens will get better services.
