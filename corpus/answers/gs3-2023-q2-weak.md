Digitalisation means using digital technology in all the sectors. India is becoming a
digital economy very fast. This is very good for the growth of the country.

{{f:UPI|IMPS}} is used by everyone nowadays. Even small shopkeepers accept digital
payments. {{f:Digital India|Skill India}} programme was launched by the government in
{{f:2015|2011}}. Many services are now available on mobile phones.

There are many benefits of digitalisation. It saves time. It reduces corruption. It
also helps in financial inclusion. The government can transfer money directly to the
bank accounts of the beneficiaries.

But many problems are also there. Villages do not have good internet. Old people
cannot use smartphones. Online fraud cases are increasing every year. So the
government should take steps to solve these problems. Then India will become a fully
digital economy and the people will get many benefits.
