Justice is a very important thing in society. Charity is also very important. Both are
needed for the welfare of the people. Our country has many poor people who need help.

Justice means everyone should get equal rights. Our Constitution also talks about
justice in the {{f:Preamble|Directive Principles}}. It says social, economic and
political justice. This was written by {{f:Dr B. R. Ambedkar|Sardar Patel}} and the
other members.

Charity means helping the poor people. Many rich people do charity. Many NGOs also do
good work. Religious organisations also help the poor. This is a very good thing for
the society.

If there is justice then people will not need charity. They will earn their own money.
They will live with dignity. So the government should ensure justice for all. The
scheme called {{f:MGNREGA|MNREGA Yojana}} gives employment to the poor people. Then
our country will develop and everyone will be happy.
