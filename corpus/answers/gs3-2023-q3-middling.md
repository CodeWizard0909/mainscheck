Artificial Intelligence is the branch of computer science which deals with machines
that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. It includes machine learning,
deep learning and natural language processing. AI systems learn from large amounts of
data instead of being programmed with fixed rules.

AI helps in clinical diagnosis in several ways. It is used for analysing medical
images such as X-rays and CT scans. It can detect diseases like
{{f:tuberculosis|typhoid}} and diabetic retinopathy at an early stage. It can also
predict the risk of diseases based on patient history. This is very useful in India
because the number of doctors is low compared to the population.

There are also concerns about privacy. Health data is highly sensitive and its misuse
can cause harm to individuals. Insurance companies or employers may use such data
against patients. Data breaches are another risk because hospitals often do not have
strong cyber security.

The {{f:Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023|Digital Personal Data Protection
Act, 2021}} has provided a framework for consent and data protection. However
implementation is important. Therefore AI should be used in healthcare with proper
regulation, informed consent and accountability of doctors for the final decision.
