Decision making is an important part of human life. Every leader has to take decisions
which affect many people. Visionary decisions are those which look far into the future
and bring long term benefits to society.

Intuition means the ability to understand something without conscious reasoning. Logic
means reasoning based on facts and evidence. Both are important for good decision
making. If a leader uses only logic then the decision may be slow and may miss new
opportunities. If a leader uses only intuition then the decision may be wrong because
it is not based on data.

There are many examples in history. The Green Revolution in
{{f:1960s|1980s}} was based on both scientific evidence and the vision of leaders like
{{f:C. Subramaniam|Verghese Kurien}}. The space programme of India also required
long term vision along with careful planning.

However, intuition without logic can also lead to failure. Many business decisions
have failed because they were based only on instinct. Therefore leaders should verify
their intuition with data before taking big decisions.

In conclusion, visionary decision making requires a balance. Intuition gives the
direction and logic gives the validation. The combination of both is very important
for leaders in government as well as in business.
