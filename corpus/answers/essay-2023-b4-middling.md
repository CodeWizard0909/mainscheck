Education is a very important part of human life. This quotation means that the real
value of education is not the information we memorise but the values and skills that
stay with us throughout our life.

In schools students learn many subjects such as mathematics, science and history.
Most of the facts are forgotten after the examination. But the ability to think
logically, to work in a team and to be disciplined remains with the student. These
qualities help in the workplace and also in personal life.

The Indian education system has been criticised for focusing too much on rote
learning. Students study only to get marks. The
{{f:National Education Policy, 2020|National Education Policy, 2010}} has therefore
recommended a shift towards conceptual understanding and critical thinking. It also
recommends reducing the importance of board examinations.

However, knowledge is also important and should not be ignored. A doctor or an
engineer cannot work without subject knowledge. Therefore both knowledge and skills
are necessary.

In conclusion, education should develop the whole personality of the student. Marks
and degrees are temporary, but values, curiosity and the ability to learn new things
are permanent. This is what remains after formal schooling is over.
