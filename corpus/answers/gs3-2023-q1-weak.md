Manufacturing is very important for the economy of any country. India is an
agricultural country but now manufacturing is also growing. MSME means Micro Small and
Medium Enterprises. They are called the backbone of the economy.

The government has started many schemes for this sector. {{f:Make in India|Digital
India}} was launched in {{f:2014|2019}}. There is also the PLI scheme which gives
incentives to the companies. Mudra loans are also given to small businesses.

MSMEs provide a lot of employment in our country. They also help in exports. They are
very important for balanced regional development. Many people in rural areas depend on
these enterprises for their livelihood.

But there are many problems also. They do not get loans easily. Technology is old.
Marketing is also a problem for them. The government should therefore help them more
so that the share of manufacturing in {{f:GDP|GNP}} can increase and the economy can
grow faster.
