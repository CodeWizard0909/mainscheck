Article 356 is a very important article of our Constitution. It is also called
President's Rule. It is used when there is a failure of constitutional machinery in a
State. It was used many times in the past.

There was a very famous case called the {{f:Bommai case|Golaknath case}}. In this case
the Supreme Court gave many important directions. The Court said that judicial review
is possible. This was a very good judgment for federalism.

Many political changes also happened in the country. Coalition governments came to
power at the Centre. Regional parties became very strong. The
{{f:Sarkaria Commission|Punchhi Commission}} also gave recommendations about Centre
State relations in {{f:1988|1998}}.

So because of all these reasons Article 356 is not used frequently now. This is good
for the federal structure of our country. It also helps in maintaining good relations
between the Centre and the States.
