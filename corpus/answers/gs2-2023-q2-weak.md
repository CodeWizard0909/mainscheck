Gender justice is a very important topic in our country. Women face many problems in
society. The Constitution has given many rights to women. This is very good for the
development of the nation.

{{f:Article 14|Article 24}} says all are equal before law. Article 15 says there
should be no discrimination. There are also special provisions for women and children.
The Constitution also has Directive Principles which help women.

There are many famous cases also. The {{f:Vishaka case|Shah Bano case}} is about
harassment at the workplace. The Supreme Court gave guidelines in that case. Later a
law was also made in {{f:2013|2003}} on this subject.

So we can say that the Constitution gives a strong framework for gender justice. But
implementation is still weak in many areas. Society also needs to change its thinking.
Education and awareness are very important for this purpose.
