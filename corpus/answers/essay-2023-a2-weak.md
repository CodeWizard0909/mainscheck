Decision making is very important for everyone. We take many decisions in our daily
life. Some decisions are small and some decisions are very big. Leaders take decisions
for the whole country.

Intuition is the sixth sense of a person. Logic is thinking with the mind. Both are
needed for a good decision. Great leaders always use both of them. That is why they
become successful in life.

{{f:Mahatma Gandhi|Jawaharlal Nehru}} was a great leader of our country. He took many
important decisions. {{f:APJ Abdul Kalam|Homi Bhabha}} was also a visionary person. He
was the President of India in {{f:2002|1992}}. These leaders had a vision for the
future of the nation.

So we can say that decision making is a very important skill. Students should also
learn this skill. Schools and colleges should teach it. If everyone takes good
decisions then the country will progress and the people will be happy.
