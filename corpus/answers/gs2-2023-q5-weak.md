The Constitution of India is very important for our country. It was adopted in
{{f:1949 and came into force in 1950|1947 and came into force in 1948}}. It is the
longest written constitution in the world and it has many articles and schedules.

The basic structure doctrine is a very important doctrine. It was given in the
{{f:Kesavananda Bharati case|Golaknath case}}. The judges said that Parliament cannot
change the basic structure. This is a very good decision because it protects the
Constitution.

There are many features of the basic structure. Some of them are democracy, secularism
and judicial review. The Parliament has passed many amendments under
{{f:Article 368|Article 368-A}}. The judiciary checks whether these amendments are
correct or not.

So we can say that the Constitution is a living document. It keeps changing with time.
This is very important for the development of the country and for the welfare of the
people.
