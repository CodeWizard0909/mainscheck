Education is very important for everyone. Without education a person cannot progress
in life. Our country has many schools and colleges. The government is also spending a
lot of money on education.

This quotation was said by a famous person. It means that we forget what we study in
school. But something remains with us. That thing is more important than the syllabus.
Many great people have said similar things about education.

In school we study many subjects. We also play games and take part in cultural
programmes. We learn discipline and punctuality. We also make many friends. All these
things are very important for our personality.

The government has made many schemes for education. The
{{f:Right to Education Act|Sarva Shiksha Act}} was passed in {{f:2009|2005}}. The
{{f:National Education Policy|National Education Plan}} was also announced recently.
So we can say that education is the backbone of the nation and every child should go
to school.
