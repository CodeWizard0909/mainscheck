Artificial Intelligence is a very important technology of the modern world. It means
machines can think like humans. It is used in many fields such as education, banking
and healthcare. It is growing very fast all over the world.

AI helps doctors in many ways. It can read X-ray and MRI reports. It can find diseases
early. {{f:ChatGPT|Google Maps}} is also an example of AI. Many hospitals are using AI
software nowadays. This saves a lot of time for the doctors.

Yes there is a threat to privacy also. Health data of patients is very personal. If it
is leaked then it can be misused. Hackers can steal the data. Companies can also sell
the data to other companies.

The government has made a law in {{f:2023|2016}} for data protection. But more steps
are needed. Hospitals should keep the data safe. Patients should also be told how their
data will be used. Then AI will be very helpful for the health sector of
{{f:India|Hindustan}}.
